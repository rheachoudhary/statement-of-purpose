When I was about eight years old, my parents decided to move from the Middle East to Australia to secure a better and brighter future for me and my brother. However, my Dad struggled to find work once we got there, so my parents made the tough decision to live separately -- my Mum with my brother and me in Australia, and my Dad overseas. For almost two decades, my Mum effectively functioned as a single parent; back in the Middle East, my Dad worked long hours to send us money and afford a few trips a year to visit, including a special trip for my 16th birthday, an occasion he usually had to miss.
